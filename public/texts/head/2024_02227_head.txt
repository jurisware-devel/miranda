SUMMARY

Statement of Case

Appeal, by permission of an Associate Judge of the Court of Appeals,
from an order of the Appellate Division of the Supreme Court in the
Second Judicial Department, entered July 6, 2022. The Appellate
Division (1) reversed, on the law, a judgment of the Supreme Court,
Queens County (Ira H. Margulis, J.), which had convicted defendant,
upon a jury verdict, of criminal possession of a weapon in the second
degree; and (2) ordered a new trial.

[2]People v Franklin, 207 AD3d 476, reversed.
