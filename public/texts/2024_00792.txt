Order affirmed. Viewing the evidence in the light most favorable to the People, "there is a valid line of reasoning and permissible inferences from which a rational jury could have found" that defendant violated Vehicle and Traffic Law § 600 (2) (a) ([3]People v Rouse, 34 NY3d 269, 274 [2019]).

Chief Judge Wilson and Judges Rivera, Garcia, Singas, Cannataro, Troutman and Halligan concur.
